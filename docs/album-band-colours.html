Wat de kopband gekleurd houdt

Three candidates on one spread. Stain gives the whole book one band in its own leather; tier colours each leaf in the metal of the cards on it; index keeps the stain and puts the tier in a three-pixel rule along the band's foot. The five stains are the published ones from albumLeather.ts, and the band is derived from --leather-mid rather than chosen — see the note in the source.

The five books

The band against the paper, the silver and the gold, in all five stains. What the derivation is for: cognac and antraciet are the two that break a naive rule — nine points of lightness apart at hi and almost together at mid.
